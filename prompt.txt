One additional endpoint

I'd also add:

POST /auth/complete-profile

Here's why:

With Google or Apple sign-in, users typically don't provide all the information your app needs. After the first login, you'll likely want to collect things like:

Phone number
User type (Customer or Artisan)
Address or city
Profile photo (optional)

A dedicated "complete profile" endpoint makes onboarding much cleaner than trying to gather everything during OAuth.

At this point, I'd stop working on database design and focus on getting authentication finished. Once auth is in place, your frontend team can begin integrating most of the app while the remaining APIs are built in parallel.
